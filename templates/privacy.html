Privacy Notice

How your resume and placement preparation data are collected, processed, and managed

This notice explains in plain language what data the AI Campus Placement Agent collects, how it is processed by third-party AI services, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it at any time.

1. What Data Is Collected

When you use the AI Campus Placement Agent, we collect and process only the information necessary to evaluate your placement readiness:

  • Resume Content: Text extracted from uploaded PDF or DOCX resume documents, or text pasted directly into the analysis form (such as education, skills, work experience, and projects).
  • Job Preferences: Target company selections and any job descriptions you provide to evaluate skill matching.
  • Mock Interview Answers: Answers you submit during mock interview practice sessions.
  • Account Details (Optional): If you register an account, your username, email address, and securely hashed password are stored for authentication.

2. Third-Party AI Processing (Gemini API)

To generate personalized placement guidance, resume text and target role details are sent to a third-party AI service:

  • Service Provider: Google Gemini API (gemini-1.5-flash or configured Gemini model) via encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Purpose: The AI model evaluates ATS compatibility, identifies missing skills, generates a 6-week study roadmap, and crafts role-specific interview practice questions.
  • PII Redaction Feature: You can optionally enable automatic Personally Identifiable Information (PII) redaction on the upload form to strip phone numbers, emails, addresses, and government IDs before sending your resume to the AI model.
Your resume text is sent to the Gemini API solely for the immediate purpose of generating your placement evaluation.

3. What Is Stored and Where

All persistent data is stored locally on the server host:

  • Local Database: Analysis summaries, ATS scores, skill gap breakdowns, 6-week study roadmaps, and mock interview transcripts are stored in a local SQLite database (data/campus_agent.db).
  • Temporary Uploads: Uploaded resume files are temporarily saved to a local uploads/ directory with a randomized UUID prefix for text extraction and validation.
  • No External Data Sharing: Stored data is never sold, shared with recruiters, or used for commercial marketing.

4. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as helpful for your campus placement preparation:

  • Analysis History: Past analyses and interview transcripts remain in the database so you can track longitudinal improvements and reopen previous dashboards without repeating AI calls.
  • Temporary Files: Uploaded resume files in uploads/ are temporary files that can be cleaned up periodically.

5. How to Delete Your Data

You have full control over your stored information and can delete it at any time:

Delete Individual Analyses

Remove a specific resume evaluation or interview transcript from the database without affecting other records.

Go to History

Erase Complete Account

Permanently delete your profile and all associated analyses, mock interviews, notes, and records.

Delete Account

6. Open Source & Project Scope

The AI Campus Placement Agent is an independently maintained, open-source educational project. It is not an enterprise data broker and makes no commercial claims. You can inspect the entire codebase, database schema, and agent pipelines on GitHub at GitHub Repository.
