Defense Rubric
Campus recruiters evaluate projects to verify authorship and problem-solving authenticity.
1. Design Choices Why did you pick this database/framework instead of alternatives? What constraints guided your choice?
2. Challenges & Bugs What was the hardest bug, concurrency race condition, or performance hurdle, and how did you diagnose it?
3. Testing & Verification How did you validate edge cases, prevent regressions, and verify input sanitization?
4. Retrospective & Improvements What architectural flaw would you redesign today with more time or budget?
5. Technical Trade-offs How did you balance latency vs simplicity, speed vs storage, or ease vs extensibility?
