Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security. In addition to your password, you will need a 6-digit code generated by an authenticator app on your phone whenever you sign in.

Password Exposure Defense Even if someone learns your password, they cannot access your account without your phone.

Placement Account Security Best Practices
  • Never share your authenticator codes or emergency recovery codes with anyone.
  • Keep your backup recovery codes stored offline or in an encrypted password vault.
  • Review your Active Sessions periodically to revoke unrecognized devices.
