How Recovery Codes Protect Your Account
  • Stored securely: We only store cryptographic salted SHA-256 hashes. Your plaintext recovery codes are never stored on our servers.
  • Immediate invalidation: Generating a new set automatically invalidates all previous recovery codes.
  • Single-use: Once a code is used to sign in, it is consumed permanently and cannot be reused.
  • One-time display: Codes are shown only once upon generation. Be sure to save or download them immediately.
